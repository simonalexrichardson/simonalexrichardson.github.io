## What I built

A small, deliberate web app — not a feature-bloated planner. The brief I set myself:

- **Capture in seconds.** If it takes longer to log a thing than it does to do it, no one will use it.
- **Shared by default.** Two people, one list. No invite flows, no role hierarchies.
- **Quietly intelligent.** Use AI where it earns its keep — parsing dates, summarising the week, surfacing patterns — and not anywhere it doesn't.
